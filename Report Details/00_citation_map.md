## Usage Guide

In your report text, place citations as superscript numbers in square brackets at the end of the relevant sentence. Examples:

> "The Transformer architecture introduced the self-attention mechanism that forms the foundation of modern LLMs [1]."

> "Large Language Models such as GPT-4 have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in language understanding and generation [3]."

> "The system uses CrewAI [5], an agent-based framework that enables autonomous task execution through the ReAct paradigm [4]."
